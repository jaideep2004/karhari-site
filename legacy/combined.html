YouTube Content ID
Policy & Rules

Fair Use. Fair Play. Protect Creativity.

Karhari Media follows all YouTube & Google policies to protect the rights of creators and ensure a fair digital environment.

What is YouTube Content ID?
YouTube Content ID is an automated system that scans uploaded videos for copyrighted material in our database. It helps protect creators by identifying unauthorized use of their content.
Audio Matching
Visual Matching
Database Scan
Ownership
YouTube Content ID Policy & Rules
You must own or have full rights to the content.
Upload only content you created or have permission to use.
Do not upload someone else's content.
Using copyrighted material without permission may lead to claims, strikes or account termination.
Respect Fair Use Guidelines.
Educational, review, commentary or news content may be eligible for fair use, but not always.
No false claims.
Misusing Content ID to claim content you don't own is a serious violation and may lead to legal action.
Comply with Community Guidelines.
Content must follow all YouTube Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
How Content ID Works
1.
Upload
A creator uploads a video to YouTube.
2.
Scan
YouTube scans the video against our Content ID database.
3.
Match Found
If a match is found, the claimant can choose an action.
4.
Action Taken
Options: Allow, Block, Monetize, or Track (View Analytics).
